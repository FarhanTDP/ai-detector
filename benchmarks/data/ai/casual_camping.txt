So this happened last weekend and I'm still kind of laughing about it, so I figured I'd share.

My buddy Jake has been bugging me for months to go camping with him. I'm not really an outdoors person. Like, my idea of roughing it is when the wifi at the hotel is slow. But he finally wore me down and I agreed to one night at a state park about two hours north of us. How bad could it be, right?

We get there Saturday afternoon and the first problem is the tent. Jake swore up and down that he knew how to set it up. He did not know how to set it up. We spent almost an hour wrestling with poles and stakes while a family of four at the next campsite watched us like we were the evening's entertainment. At one point the dad actually walked over and asked if we needed help. Jake said no. We definitely needed help.

Eventually we got it standing, sort of. It was leaning a little to the left but whatever, it was up. Then we tried to start a fire. Jake brought this fancy fire starter thing he bought online, and it turns out the wood we collected was still damp from the rain earlier in the week. So we're sitting there blowing on these tiny sad sparks for like twenty minutes. The family next door had a roaring fire going and were literally roasting marshmallows. I'm not proud of it, but I walked over and asked if we could borrow a couple of logs. They were super nice about it and even gave us some marshmallows.

The real kicker came around two in the morning. I woke up because something was rustling right outside the tent. My heart was pounding. I'm thinking bear, mountain lion, serial killer, all the options. I shake Jake awake and we're both lying there frozen, listening. Finally Jake grabs his flashlight, unzips the tent super slowly, and shines it outside.

It was a raccoon. Just one fat raccoon, sitting on top of our cooler, eating the hot dogs we forgot to put in the car. It looked right at us, totally unbothered, and kept eating.

Anyway, we packed up early the next morning, stopped at a diner for pancakes, and drove home. Jake is already talking about going again next month. Honestly? I might actually say yes. But this time I'm bringing a lock for the cooler.
