My cat has started attending my work meetings, and I think she's about to be promoted over me.

It began innocently. Three weeks ago, Biscuit jumped onto my desk during the Monday standup and sat directly in front of the webcam. I apologized and moved her. She came back. I moved her again. By the third attempt my manager said, "Just let her stay, she clearly has something to contribute," and everybody laughed, and that was the beginning of the end.

She now arrives two minutes before every call, which is more than I can say for Greg from accounting. She sits upright, slightly to my left, with the calm and faintly disappointed expression of someone who has read the quarterly numbers and has concerns.

Last Wednesday I was presenting a slide about customer retention. Midway through, Biscuit yawned, hugely, with all her teeth. Our director unmuted and said, "I agree with the cat, can we skip to the recommendations?" I skipped to the recommendations. The cat received credit for improving the efficiency of the meeting.

It has only escalated from there. Someone made her a custom emoji. She has been added, as a joke, to the team's birthday spreadsheet, and then, not as a joke, to a planning document, where a task was assigned to "Biscuit (pending availability)." Yesterday a colleague messaged me privately to ask whether Biscuit would be on the two o'clock, because if not, she'd probably skip it.

I want to be clear that Biscuit does nothing. She has never opened a spreadsheet. She does not understand our product. Her one independent action in the past month was to walk across my keyboard and send the letter "j" forty-one times to a client, who replied, "Finally, some honesty."

And yet. People listen when she blinks. She has the kind of quiet authority I have spent nine years and two leadership courses trying to develop. She never over-explains. She never says "just circling back." When a meeting runs long, she simply leaves, and nobody questions it.

My performance review is next month. I've been advised to bring her.
